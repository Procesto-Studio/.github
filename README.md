# Procesto · AI Automation Studio

**We turn manual business processes into reliable automations: from the first inquiry to the manager's next action.**

Procesto is a small automation studio in Poland working with small and mid-sized businesses across the EU. We build with n8n, connect CRMs, forms, mail and messengers, and add AI only where it saves real time. People stay in control of every decision that matters.

**Who we work with:** consulting firms, relocation and legalization agencies, online schools, service businesses.
**Languages:** Ukrainian · Polish · English · Russian
